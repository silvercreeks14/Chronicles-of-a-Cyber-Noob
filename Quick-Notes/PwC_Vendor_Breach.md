# Third-Party Risk in Action: The PwC Vendor Breach

- **What happened:** On October 22, 2026, the Everest ransomware group claimed to have stolen ~590 GB of data (over 100,000 files) from the global consulting firm PwC. PwC confirmed an incident the following day, clarifying that their own network was not breached. Instead, the attackers compromised a third-party vendor that PwC used to host employee learning and training records. 
- **Why it caught my attention:** It shows that even massive firms with elite cybersecurity teams are vulnerable to supply chain attacks. The attackers didn't need to break into PwC's highly secured primary network; they just needed to find a weaker, secondary vendor holding PwC's data. 
- **The Takeaway:** Securing your own perimeter is no longer enough. Organizations must rigorously vet and monitor their third-party vendors because a breach anywhere in the supply chain becomes a breach of your own data.
